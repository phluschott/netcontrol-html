MY NETS

Set up your net once. Enter the name, schedule, repeater, preamble, standing announcements, and (optionally) your regulars.

After that, each week is: Start Net → preamble → check-ins → announcements → round of comments → late check-ins → closing. New stations who check in are remembered for next week.
